How it works

Three steps to funded.

Simple paperwork, a real person on the other end, and straight answers on cost.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Send a short request with the equipment, the cost and a little about your business.

  2. Review your options

    We match you with the right structure and walk you through payments, terms and buyout options.

  3. Get your equipment

    Sign the documents, we pay the vendor, and your equipment goes to work.
