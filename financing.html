Financing options

The right structure for every purchase.

Leases, loans and programs built around the equipment, your cash flow and your plans for it.

Equipment leaseEquipment loanSale-leasebackUsed and private partyVendor programsStart-ups and growing businesses
Equipment lease

Lower payments, flexible end-of-term choices.

Use the equipment for a set term with a fixed monthly payment. At the end, buy it, return it or upgrade.

  • Little or no money down
  • Choice of buyout options
  • Easy to upgrade as you grow
Best for: Equipment you plan to replace or upgrade
Equipment loan

Own it from day one.

Finance the purchase and own the equipment outright. The equipment itself secures the loan.

  • You own the equipment
  • Fixed rate and payment
  • Build equity with every payment
Best for: Long-life equipment you plan to keep
Sale-leaseback

Turn equipment you own into cash.

Sell equipment you already own and lease it back. You keep using it and free up capital.

  • Unlock cash from paid-off equipment
  • No interruption to operations
  • Use funds for growth or debt payoff
Best for: Businesses with valuable equipment and a need for cash
Used and private party

Good equipment does not have to be new.

Financing for used equipment from dealers, auctions and private sellers.

  • Dealer, auction or private seller
  • Inspection-based approvals
  • Stretch your budget further
Best for: Buyers who want value without waiting
Vendor programs

Help your customers say yes.

Equipment sellers can offer financing at the point of sale and close more deals.

  • Fast decisions for your customers
  • You get paid in full
  • Co-branded options available
Best for: Dealers, distributors and manufacturers
Start-ups and growing businesses

Newer business? Let's talk.

Options for companies with a shorter track record, built around the equipment and the plan.

  • Programs for newer businesses
  • Equipment-focused approvals
  • Room to grow into larger lines
Best for: Companies with less than two years in business
